\section{A figure}

Put image files in \texttt{figures/} and include them like this:

\begin{verbatim}
\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \includegraphics[width=0.6\textwidth]{my-image.png}
  \caption{My caption.}
  \label{fig:my-image}
\end{figure}
\end{verbatim}
